Ask a Question
What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities vary by bottle size and delivery location. Share your requirement through the enquiry form and our team will confirm the minimum for your area.

Do you offer recurring supply for offices?

Yes. We can arrange daily, weekly or monthly supply based on your team size and consumption. Your account manager will help define a schedule that avoids running out.

How quickly can a delivery be arranged?

For most service areas, deliveries can be scheduled within the same week. Urgent requirements are handled on a case-by-case basis depending on stock and routing.

Can I request a custom bottle size or label?

We currently offer standard bottle sizes listed on our products page. For custom labelling or large corporate orders, contact us to discuss what’s possible.
